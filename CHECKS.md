# Vertex — Milestone 12 manual review

Use a published competition with one registered individual, one registered team, and another registered participant who will not be assigned. Open organiser and participant accounts in separate browser windows.

- [ ] From the organiser’s competition workspace, open **Manage meetings**. Confirm the creation form and scheduled meeting list are easy to understand.
- [ ] Enter a meeting name and start time. Search for the individual by name or @username and for the team by name. Select both; confirm each appears in the selected list and can be removed.
- [ ] Create the meeting. Confirm the success message appears, the meeting is listed, and **View assigned people** shows the chosen participant and team.
- [ ] Try creating another meeting with the same name but different capitalisation. Confirm Vertex explains that the name is already used.
- [ ] As the assigned individual and each team member, check **Notifications** and the competition’s **Meetings** page. Confirm the meeting appears with the correct local date and time.
- [ ] As the unassigned participant, open the **Meetings** page and paste the meeting’s direct address. Confirm the meeting is absent from the list and the direct page says it is unavailable; no Jitsi call should appear.
- [ ] As an assigned person, open the meeting’s direct address, refresh it, and wait for the Jitsi prejoin screen. Confirm the meeting fills the available screen and **Leave meeting** returns to the competition page.
- [ ] Repeat the list and meeting route on a phone width and desktop width. Check that names, buttons, Jitsi controls, and the organiser roster remain readable without horizontal scrolling. Review light and dark mode.
- [ ] Use the keyboard to search, select, remove, create, open, and leave a meeting. Confirm focus is visible and loading or connection errors are explained in text. Repeat with reduced motion enabled.

If a check fails, note the page address, account role, device width, theme, and action taken.
