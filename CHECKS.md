# Vertex: manual UX review

Use your existing organiser, participant and organisation accounts. These checks
can be done in one sitting. No competition date changes or new uploads are needed.
Use a phone and desktop if available; repeat key screens in light and dark mode.

## Navigation and first impressions

- [ ] Log out, then log in normally as each role. Confirm Dashboard opens first
      and is the first navigation item. Refresh; confirm your session remains.
- [ ] Open a protected page while logged out, then log in. Confirm you return to
      that page. Use the header logo to visit the landing page; no separate Home
      link should appear in navigation or the footer.
- [ ] As an organiser, click the blank area, title and arrow of a competition
      card. Each should open the same workspace. With Tab and Enter, confirm the
      card is one understandable link, with a visible focus outline.
- [ ] Open the account menu beside your name. Check Edit profile/organisation,
      public profile and Log out are clear. Press Escape; focus should return to
      the trigger. Click outside; it should close.
- [ ] On a phone, open the navigation drawer. Confirm Dashboard comes first,
      account editing and logout are available, and closing restores scrolling.
      Open a competition and choose sections from Competition section. Confirm
      the picker shows the current section after a refresh.
- [ ] Reload a page with network throttling if you want to inspect startup.
      The summit mark should feel connected to Vertex; loading should finish as
      soon as the page is ready. Enable reduced motion in device settings and
      confirm the page remains usable without animation.

## Forms, hierarchy and feedback

- [ ] Open profile settings, competition creation, submission settings and
      judging. Compare text fields, selects, search and date/time inputs:
      consistent borders, corners, labels and focus. Native colour/file controls
      may differ because they have different jobs.
- [ ] Submit an empty login form. Follow an error-summary link and correct that
      field. Confirm the field receives focus and the error clears when valid.
- [ ] In competition registration, inspect the person row and field tags.
      Expect Age, Programming and General STEM; usernames keep their spelling.
- [ ] Open an overview and its leaderboard. Check consistent section navigation,
      heading size and space before content. Registration explanations should
      sit close to their heading rather than occupy a large empty panel.
- [ ] Edit a profile description without saving. Try another page, then cancel
      the discard prompt. Confirm your text remains. Restore the original text
      before leaving if you do not intend to save it.
- [ ] On your phone with the keyboard open, edit a description, date/time and
      search query. Confirm focus, validation and actions remain reachable
      without sideways scrolling.
- [ ] At 200% browser zoom, inspect a dense organiser page. Then inspect it in
      dark mode and bright light. Text, selected states and focus must stay clear.
- [ ] With your screen reader, inspect labels/help text, the account disclosure,
      competition section picker and validation summary. Confirm names and
      current selections are announced understandably.

## Your populated competition workflows

These need your normal data. The automated visual audit used accounts with no
registered entries, scores or certificate artwork; it did not fabricate those states.

- [ ] In an existing team competition, inspect captain/member rows, pending
      invitations and team-size feedback. Long names should wrap without hiding actions.
- [ ] Open an assigned meeting on your device. Confirm join/leave and return to
      competition are clear. An unassigned account must not join.
- [ ] Review existing submissions and judging rows. Check long filenames,
      missing submissions, unfinished scores and validation stay understandable.
      Cancel navigation while scores are unsaved; confirm your work remains.
- [ ] Open a published leaderboard and previous round. Confirm searching,
      advancement labels and visible/hidden scores agree with organiser settings.
      Completed cards should prioritise results over old deadlines.
- [ ] When using your certificate template, inspect long names, dragging,
      resizing, keyboard controls and the downloaded PDF. No template upload or
      live certificate generation was repeated during this audit.
- [ ] Open Vertex as an installed app. Confirm title-bar theme, safe-area spacing,
      update feedback and install/push instructions on your physical device.

## Optional draft deletion check

- [ ] Create a disposable unpublished draft with no invitations or entries.
      On your phone, open Delete draft, type its exact saved name, then choose
      Keep draft. Confirm the draft and unsaved edits remain.
- [ ] Only if you want to test permanent deletion, reopen that disposable draft's
      dialog and choose Delete permanently. Confirm it leaves your dashboard and
      its old address reports Competition unavailable. Never use a needed
      competition for this check. Published competitions retain their history.
